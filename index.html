Product Overview & Identity

Octahydrocyclopenta[b]pyrrole-2-carboxylic acid is a saturated bicyclic proline analogue: a pyrrolidine-2-carboxylic acid fused cis to a cyclopentane ring. It has three stereocentres (C2, C3a, C6a), so it exists as eight stereoisomers. The commercially relevant one is the cis-endo (2S,3aS,6aS) isomer, also called (1S,3S,5S)- or (S,S,S)-2-azabicyclo[3.3.0]octane-3-carboxylic acid. The (2S,3aS,6aS) acid and its esters are the key intermediates for ramipril [US 8,263,784]. Industry almost always trades it as the benzyl ester hydrochloride. The benzyl group protects the acid during peptide coupling and is later removed by Pd/C hydrogenolysis [US 7,935,831].

Do not confuse with octahydrocyclopenta[c]pyrrole-1-carboxylic acid, a different ring-fusion isomer used in HCV protease inhibitor work [RCSB], or with octahydroindole-2-carboxylic acid (perindopril/trandolapril, CAS 80875-98-5) [PubChem]. Always specify CAS and stereochemistry on POs.

Downstream use (customer process): the (S,S,S) benzyl ester·HCl and NEPA are coupled (Et3N/EtOAc, typically with DCC-type activation) to give ramipril benzyl ester, which is hydrogenated over Pd/C in ethanol [US 7,960,558]. DCC use creates the dicyclohexylurea (DCU) process impurity behind the 2026 Hikma recall (S05). This is an angle for a "DCU-free" free-acid supply that couples via the NEPA N-carboxyanhydride (Route 5).

Industry Applications, End-Products & Application Market Sizes

A. Pharmaceutical intermediate — Ramipril API (≈95%+ of volume MI)

Ramipril is a long-acting ACE-inhibitor prodrug, hydrolysed in the liver to ramiprilat. It is used for hypertension, heart failure after MI, and cardiovascular risk reduction. This bicyclic acid is ramipril's defining structural unit; each kg of ramipril needs ~0.68 kg of benzyl ester·HCl at 100% yield, or ~0.85–0.95 kg at typical plant yields MI calc: MW 281.78 / 416.52 ÷ 0.72–0.80 yield.

Feasibility Summary & Management Recommendation

CONDITIONAL
The product has steady, non-cyclical demand tied to a top-5 cardiovascular generic, unrestricted regulation and a natural India customer base. However, the merchant pool is small (est.
